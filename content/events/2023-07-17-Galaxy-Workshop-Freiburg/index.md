**Galaxy workshop on HTS Data analysis**

The [European Galaxy Team in Freiburg](https://usegalaxy-eu.github.io/people) is offering a Galaxy beginner workshop on high-throughput data analysis in-person (not hybrid, not online) for researchers. The workshop is a hands-on workshop and starts with a Galaxy introduction day followed by 4 days of analysis of different types of biological data. We will build this training course on the [GTN training material](https://training.galaxyproject.org/) and use the GTN tutorials with a guided step-by-step instruction and demostrations. 
Within the application form (see below), desired topics can be selected (e.g. RNA-Seq data analysis, ChIP-Seq data analysis, Variant Calling, Genome Assembly) and we will create a program out of the wish list. The final program will be presented her mid of June 2023. 

The places in the workshop are limited. Please apply for a place with the application form below. We will select the participants and inform you about participants selection until end June 2023.

* **Date:** 17-21th July 2023
* **Time:** 9-17:00
* **Location:** University of Freiburg, Biology Department
* **Application:** Use [this form](https://forms.gle/k2H3nxtVjDsqD6HQ8) to apply for a place in the workshop

If you are interested or need more information, please contact Anika Erxleben (erxleben@informatik.uni-freiburg.de).
